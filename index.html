🔥 Ember Foundry

HOW TO PLAY
  • Tap the anvil to strike it and earn gold.
  • Spend gold on stations — they keep earning gold per second even while you're away.
  • Buy upgrades and boosts to multiply your output.
  • Once you've earned enough, Rebirth for a permanent bonus that carries into your next run.
  • The game autosaves in this browser, so closing the tab is safe.
⚠️ FIXED: a bug where spam-clicking the anvil very fast could pile up so many spark/ember effects at once that the page would slow down or freeze. The anvil now has a short overheat cooldown.
